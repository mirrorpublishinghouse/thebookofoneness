THE MYTH OF FAIRNESS

06BUCKET · TERM 5/7

The Story that outcomes can be balanced, measured, or deserved. Fairness is not contact. It is The Mind Narrating distribution as justice to preserve Identity against loss. The Myth of Fairness converts chance into order, hierarchy into virtue, and Suffering into lesson. It disguises inequality as correction, casting reward and punishment as evidence of balance. But there is no balance. No scale. Only Oneness misread as transaction.
