THE PULSE

02BUCKET · TERM 3/11

The first tremble within Formlessness. The Pulse is not movement, not sequence, and not origin. It is the undivided rhythm of Oneness in silent release. It does not begin. It does not arrive. It is the spontaneous stirring of pressure within the seamless: not aimed, not caused. The Pulse does not push through space. It vibrates through Everything. It is the rhythm before Form. Motion before direction. Presence unable to remain still.
