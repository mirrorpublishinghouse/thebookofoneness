KNOWING

10BUCKET · TERM 3/7

A direct, experiential Recognition of Oneness, including The Mind, The Body, and The Ground of Being. Not knowledge. Not Belief. Not proof. Knowing is contact without distance. It flashes, recedes, returns. It cannot be claimed. It dissolves as The Illusion of Separation reasserts itself, but it is never absent.
