THE STORY OF SUPERIORITY

04BUCKET · TERM 3/5

The Narrative that assigns greater worth to specific Forms. It disguises hierarchy as nature, dominance as virtue, and Role as justification. The Story of Superiority preserves inequality by embedding Belief into Myth, then rehearsing that Myth through Systems and Masks. This Story is how The Delusion of Power fortifies itself. There is no higher. No chosen. Only Oneness, misperceived and misnamed.
