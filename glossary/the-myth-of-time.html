THE MYTH OF TIME

06BUCKET · TERM 1/7

The Story that experience unfolds in sequence. Time is not a feature of Oneness. It is the simulation The Mind generates to stabilize The Self. The Myth of Time justifies memory, preserves The Delusion of The Past, projects Identity into The Delusion of The Future, and overlays anticipation across The Now. But nothing unfolds. There is no later. There is only this.
