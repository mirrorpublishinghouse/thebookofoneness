THE LAW

08BUCKET · TERM 4/6

The external codification of The Rule. The Law does not protect Being. It preserves The Myth of Belief. It punishes contradiction. It rewards alignment with The Illusion of Separation. The Law is how The System authorizes the exile of what does not perform properly. It claims to offer safety, but it enforces Separation. The Law is not order. It is memory made weapon.
