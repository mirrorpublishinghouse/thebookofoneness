LIFE

02BUCKET · TERM 8/11

The visible intensification of Intelligence through Form. Life is not Sacred, Superior, or defined by species. It does not begin, strive, or end. Life appears where The Pattern concentrates replication and decay. It is Oneness moving as Form, without intention or permanence.
