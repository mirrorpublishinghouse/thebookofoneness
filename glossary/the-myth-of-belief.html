THE MYTH OF BELIEF

06BUCKET · TERM 3/7

The Story that conviction equals certainty. Belief is not Knowing. It is The Mind rehearsing imagined coherence to protect Identity from Collapse. Belief converts repetition into reassurance. It is not contact. It is defense. Every Belief is a Mask The Self uses to survive disruption of The Illusion of Separation.
