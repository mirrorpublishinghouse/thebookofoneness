NOW / THE PRESENT

01BUCKET · TERM 2/2

The undivided immediacy of Oneness. It is not a moment, not a feeling, and not a state of Presence. It cannot pass, be approached, or be returned to. It does not unfold. It is what remains when the projection of The Myth of Time is no longer Believed.
