FRACTURE

09BUCKET · TERM 1/2

The first breach in The Illusion of Separation. Fracture is not insight. Not revelation. It is the failure of The Mind to maintain The Narrative of continuity. It appears as rupture, panic, silence, awe, illness, or disorder, but it is not Collapse. It is the split where rehearsal stutters. Where The Loop is interrupted. Fracture interrupts performance. It does not liberate. It exposes. Every Fracture opens space for Recognition. It is how The Great Remembering begins and how the apparatus of The Illusion of Separation begins to weaken before Collapse.
