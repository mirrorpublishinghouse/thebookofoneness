THE LOOP

03BUCKET · TERM 4/13

The recursive thought sequence generated by The Mind to preserve continuity. It is not awareness. It is not observation. The Loop is not memory or behavior, it is the repetition of neural activity reinforced by perception, reaction, and Narrative rehearsal. It begins as interpretation. It hardens into Meaning and then Belief. It is not The Pattern. It is the simulation of rhythm, not its source. The Loop sustains The Self by reactivating familiar Stories. It adapts to survive Collapse. It Narrates in your voice. But it is not you. It must be interrupted before it can Collapse.
