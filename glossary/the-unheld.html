THE UNHELD

10BUCKET · TERM 6/7

Not peace. Not rest. Not stillness. The Unheld is what remains when The Grip of Attachment has dissolved. It is motion without rehearsal. Form without Story. Sensation without anticipation. It is not refuge. It is not silence. It is what remains after Release, untouched by The Illusion of Separation.
