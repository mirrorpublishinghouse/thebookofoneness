IDENTITY

03BUCKET · TERM 6/13

The adaptive casing The Mind constructs to stabilize The Self. It is not Being. It is not what moves. Identity arises from Role, memory, preference, and performance shaped to match what The System rewards and what The Self resists. Identity is not fixed. It reshapes to survive. It is not your expression. It is what The Mind uses to preserve The Illusion of Separation. It does not remain when Collapse begins.
