THE GREAT REMEMBERING

10BUCKET · TERM 1/7

The re-emergence of direct Recognition of Oneness. Not return. Not insight. The Great Remembering is not a single event. It recurs whenever The Illusion of Separation loosens. It does not stabilize The Self. It interrupts it, exposing what remains beneath repetition.
