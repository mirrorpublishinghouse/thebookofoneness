THE MYTH OF PROGRESS

06BUCKET · TERM 7/7

The Story that movement equals improvement. It reframes domination as evolution and disguises repetition as becoming. It is how The Self Narrates survival as success. But nothing is advancing. There is no forthcoming state to reach. There is only The Ache of The Now, misread as unfinished.
