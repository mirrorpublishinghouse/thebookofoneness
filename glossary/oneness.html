ONENESS

01BUCKET · TERM 1/2

The undivided totality before all perception. It is not a state, not an Identity, not Presence, not a creation, and not a source. Oneness cannot be entered, understood, or experienced. It has never been absent. It is what remains when all Forms, The Illusion of Separation, Stories, Myths, and Delusions dissolve.
